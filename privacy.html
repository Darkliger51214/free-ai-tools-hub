Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Information we collect and why

The visitor counter creates a random identifier in your browser's sessionStorage for the current tab session and sends it to Supabase with a visit timestamp so the site can count visits. Supabase stores these counter records. The identifier is used for this counter; the site does not use it to create an account or a cross-site identity profile.

The site does not provide visitor accounts or contact forms. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include and use it to reply or address your request. Please do not email passwords, payment details, or information you do not want us to receive.

Service providers and external links

Netlify hosts this site, Supabase provides the visitor-counter database, and Google Fonts supplies the Inter typeface. When your browser connects to these services, their providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request information to deliver and protect their services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy notices.

Links to AI products and model repositories take you to third-party websites. Those providers have their own terms and privacy practices. Review them before creating an account, uploading content, or sharing personal or confidential information. Free AI Tools Hub does not control third-party websites.

Advertising, cookies, and your choices

The Google AdSense publisher script is installed on this site. Loading a page with this script connects your browser to Google and may transmit technical request information, such as your IP address, browser/device details, and the page requested, under Google's privacy practices. In our latest site check, Google's ad request was unfilled and no visible ad was rendered. Ad availability can change according to account settings, eligibility, and available inventory. If Auto ads are enabled in the AdSense account, Google may place ads without separate ad-unit markup on the page. The visitor counter uses sessionStorage as described above; that browser storage is separate from advertising cookies.

If advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your device. Depending on the provider, your location, and your choices, this may include cookie or advertising identifiers, IP address, browser or device information, and information about interactions with this site or ads. These technologies may be used to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated ads, detect fraud, or personalize ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use information from your visits to this site and other sites to select or personalize ads, subject to their own policies and your settings.

If ads are enabled, we will update this policy and, where legally required, ask for your consent before non-essential advertising cookies or similar technologies are used. You can manage Google's ad personalization settings at Google Ads Settings, learn how Google uses information from partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and review Google's Privacy Policy. You may also manage interest-based advertising through DAA WebChoices. Your browser may offer additional controls for cookies and site data.

Retention and policy updates

Visitor-counter records are kept in the counter database for the operation of the site statistics. Emails are kept for as long as needed to respond and handle related follow-up. We may update this policy when site practices or services change; the date above shows when it was last revised.
